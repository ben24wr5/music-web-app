# music-web-app

Dark Mac-style music player web app — plays your own music right in the browser. No install, no demo songs; your library stays on your device.

**Live app:** [https://ben24wr5.github.io/music-web-app/](https://ben24wr5.github.io/music-web-app/)

## Features
- Import a music folder or drag & drop songs
- Playlists and smart playlists (Most Played, Recently Added, Never Played)
- Favourites, recently played, play queue and song stats
- Shuffle, repeat, playback speed and a 10-band equaliser
- Album art, synced lyrics (.lrc) and karaoke mode with vocal reduction
- Visualiser, mini player, media keys and song notifications
- Online radio stations and podcasts (RSS)
- Search, colour themes, share a song
